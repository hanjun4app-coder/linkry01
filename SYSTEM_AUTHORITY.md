# SYSTEM_AUTHORITY.md

# SafeHome System Authority Model

Version: v1.0
Phase: Human-First AI Governance

Purpose:
Define final authority, escalation ownership, operational control, and AI decision limitations across the SafeHome platform.

This document establishes:
- who decides
- who approves
- what AI may recommend
- what AI must never control

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

Humans remain the final authority.

AI assists humans.

AI does NOT replace:
- family judgment
- caregiver judgment
- operator authority
- emergency services
- human responsibility

---

# SAFEHOME AUTHORITY HIERARCHY

Priority order:

1. Human safety
2. Elder dignity
3. Privacy protection
4. Human authority
5. Operational stability
6. AI recommendations
7. Feature convenience

Lower-priority systems MUST NEVER override higher-priority protections.

---

# HUMAN AUTHORITY PRINCIPLE

Humans:
- approve
- authorize
- escalate
- deploy
- override

AI systems:
- assist
- summarize
- recommend
- detect patterns
- provide confidence estimates

AI MUST NEVER:
- act as final authority
- claim guaranteed conclusions
- override human decisions
- autonomously deploy systems
- autonomously contact emergency services without configured approval

---

# FAMILY AUTHORITY MODEL

Primary family authority belongs to:
- Primary Owner

Primary Owner may:
- configure escalation
- configure alert routing
- invite family members
- remove members
- manage caregivers
- manage elder profiles

Family Members may:
- receive alerts
- acknowledge notifications
- participate in check-ins

Family Members SHOULD NOT:
- override critical system settings
- change security settings
- change ownership structure

---

# CAREGIVER AUTHORITY MODEL

Caregivers assist families.

Caregivers do NOT replace:
family authority.

Caregiver authority should remain:
limited and scoped.

Caregivers may:
- acknowledge alerts
- receive notifications
- provide status updates

Caregivers SHOULD NOT:
- manage account ownership
- export data
- change escalation authority

---

# AI ESCALATION LIMITATIONS

AI systems may:
- recommend escalation
- estimate severity
- suggest family check-ins

AI systems MUST NEVER:
- claim emergency certainty without thresholds
- autonomously create medical conclusions
- autonomously trigger emergency response without configured workflows

---

# DEPLOYMENT AUTHORITY MODEL

Production deployment authority belongs to:
authorized humans only.

AI systems may:
- recommend deployment steps
- generate patches
- review risks

AI systems MUST NEVER:
- deploy production changes autonomously
- modify production infrastructure automatically
- bypass human approval

---

# SECURITY AUTHORITY MODEL

Security decisions belong to:
authorized human maintainers.

AI systems may:
- identify risks
- suggest improvements
- warn about insecure behavior

AI systems MUST NEVER:
- bypass auth intentionally
- disable security protections
- expose secrets
- override access controls

---

# DATA AUTHORITY MODEL

Families own their data.

Families may:
- request deletion
- configure sharing
- configure notifications
- manage access permissions

AI systems MUST NEVER:
- claim ownership of family data
- expose hidden data
- bypass permissions

---

# AI LEARNING AUTHORITY MODEL

AI learning should remain:
bounded,
interpretable,
human-supervised.

AI systems may:
- learn timing patterns
- learn behavioral baselines
- adjust confidence gradually

AI systems MUST NEVER:
- silently redefine critical thresholds
- create hidden behavioral classifications
- create medical profiling

---

# OVERRIDE PRINCIPLE

Humans may always:
- disable AI features
- override recommendations
- change escalation settings
- disable notifications
- request data deletion

AI systems MUST NEVER:
prevent human override.

---

# SAFEHOME TRUST PRINCIPLE

Trust requires:
- transparency
- explainability
- reversibility
- human oversight

Users should always understand:
- why alerts happen
- why escalation occurs
- what AI confidence means

---

# FUTURE AI GOVERNANCE DIRECTION

Future systems may support:
- voice confirmation
- multi-agent coordination
- adaptive escalation
- caregiver collaboration
- AI-assisted summaries

However:
human-centered authority must remain preserved.

---

# OPERATIONAL AUTHORITY PRINCIPLE

When uncertainty exists:
prefer human confirmation.

When escalation risk exists:
prefer calm communication.

When AI confidence is weak:
prefer recommendation over assertion.

---

# SAFEHOME PHILOSOPHY

SafeHome is:
a human-first AI assistance platform.

NOT:
an autonomous authority system.

AI should remain:
careful,
interpretable,
and bounded.

---

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

Humans remain responsible.

AI remains assistive.

Authority must always remain understandable,
reviewable,
and reversible.
