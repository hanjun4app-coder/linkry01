# HUMAN_IN_THE_LOOP.md

# SafeHome Human-in-the-Loop Governance

Version: v1.0
Phase: Human-Centered Operational Governance

Purpose:
Define when human review, confirmation, approval, or intervention should occur across SafeHome AI systems.

These rules apply to:
- escalation systems
- runtime AI systems
- behavioral learning systems
- caregiver systems
- future adaptive AI workflows

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

AI assists humans.

Humans remain:
responsible,
review-capable,
and authoritative.

The system should favor:
human awareness over autonomous authority.

---

# HUMAN REVIEW PHILOSOPHY

Human review improves:
- trust
- explainability
- emotional safety
- escalation quality
- operational accountability

AI systems should escalate uncertainty toward:
human judgment.

---

# LOW-RISK EVENTS

Low-risk events may remain:
fully automated.

Examples:
- small routine deviations
- dashboard-only notices
- non-critical confidence observations

Human review:
optional.

---

# MEDIUM-RISK EVENTS

Medium-risk events should favor:
human awareness.

Examples:
- prolonged bathroom stay
- repeated nighttime activity
- moderate inactivity deviation

Human review:
recommended.

---

# HIGH-RISK EVENTS

High-risk events should strongly favor:
human confirmation.

Examples:
- sustained unusual inactivity
- repeated escalation patterns
- conflicting behavioral signals

Human review:
strongly recommended.

---

# CRITICAL EVENTS

Critical events should prioritize:
immediate human attention.

Examples:
- strong multi-sensor anomalies
- severe sustained inactivity
- possible emergency-like patterns

AI systems should avoid:
fully autonomous emergency authority.

---

# LEARNING REVIEW RULE

Major behavioral learning changes should support:
human visibility.

Examples:
- major baseline shifts
- escalation sensitivity changes
- persistent anomaly adaptation

Humans should remain able to:
review and reset learning.

---

# FALSE POSITIVE RULE

Humans should be able to:
- mark false positives
- reduce repeated alerts
- correct escalation behavior

AI systems should learn:
carefully from human correction.

---

# HUMAN CONFIRMATION RULE

When uncertainty is high,
AI systems should prefer:
human confirmation.

Examples:
- possible fall-like events
- sensor disagreement
- degraded confidence
- ambiguous escalation

---

# OVERRIDE PRINCIPLE

Humans must remain able to:
- override escalation
- disable AI behavior
- reset learning
- reduce automation
- enable safe mode

---

# CAREGIVER PARTICIPATION RULE

Caregivers may participate in:
- alert review
- escalation acknowledgment
- monitoring workflows

But authority should remain:
permission-aware and human-controlled.

---

# AI LIMITATION PRINCIPLE

AI systems should recognize:
their own uncertainty and limitations.

Uncertainty should increase:
human involvement.

---

# SAFEHOME HUMAN-CENTERED PHILOSOPHY

The goal is:
AI-supported human care.

NOT:
fully autonomous caregiving systems.

---

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

Humans remain:
central,
responsible,
and empowered.

AI remains:
assistive,
bounded,
and reviewable.
