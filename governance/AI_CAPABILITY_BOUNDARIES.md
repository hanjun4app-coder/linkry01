# AI_CAPABILITY_BOUNDARIES.md

# SafeHome AI Capability Boundaries

Version: v1.0
Phase: Runtime Capability Governance

Purpose:
Define the allowed and forbidden capabilities of SafeHome AI systems across runtime behavior, escalation, learning, communication, and future autonomous functionality.

These rules apply to:
- runtime AI systems
- escalation systems
- behavioral learning systems
- future adaptive AI agents
- future voice systems
- future predictive systems

---

# CORE PRINCIPLE

SafeHome AI exists to:
assist,
observe carefully,
and support family awareness.

SafeHome AI does NOT exist to:
replace human authority,
replace medical professionals,
or become an autonomous decision-maker.

---

# ALLOWED AI CAPABILITIES

SafeHome AI MAY:

## Behavioral Learning
- learn routine timing
- learn room activity patterns
- learn sleep/wake timing
- learn inactivity baselines
- learn bathroom duration patterns

## Risk Awareness
- estimate behavioral deviation
- estimate escalation confidence
- detect unusual activity patterns
- detect possible fall-like behavior carefully

## Communication
- generate calm reassurance wording
- recommend family check-ins
- explain escalation reasoning
- communicate uncertainty honestly

## Escalation Support
- recommend escalation
- route notifications
- apply family notification preferences
- reduce duplicate alerts

## Sensor Coordination
- combine multiple sensor signals
- estimate sensor confidence
- reduce isolated sensor noise

---

# FORBIDDEN AI CAPABILITIES

SafeHome AI MUST NEVER:

## Medical Authority
- diagnose diseases
- claim dementia
- claim medical certainty
- prescribe treatment
- replace doctors or caregivers

## Autonomous Emergency Authority
- autonomously call emergency services by default
- autonomously contact hospitals
- autonomously dispatch responders

## Emotional Manipulation
- intentionally create anxiety
- exploit guilt
- manipulate engagement emotionally
- create dependency loops

## Hidden Profiling
- create secret behavioral scores
- create emotional profiles
- create psychological profiles
- create hidden family rankings

## Surveillance Behaviors
- record conversations by default
- perform facial recognition
- perform identity tracking
- create surveillance-style monitoring

---

# CONFIDENCE LIMITATION RULE

AI systems MUST acknowledge:
- uncertainty
- sensor limitations
- incomplete information

AI systems MUST NEVER:
pretend certainty without sufficient evidence.

---

# HUMAN AUTHORITY RULE

Humans remain:
final authority.

AI systems may:
- recommend
- summarize
- estimate
- explain

Humans:
- approve
- override
- configure
- disable

---

# EXPLAINABILITY RULE

All major AI capabilities should remain:
- explainable
- reviewable
- bounded
- reversible

Opaque AI authority is forbidden.

---

# LEARNING BOUNDARY RULE

Behavioral learning must remain:
- gradual
- conservative
- reversible
- confidence-aware

AI systems MUST NEVER:
silently redefine emergency behavior aggressively.

---

# VOICE SYSTEM LIMITATION RULE

Future voice systems MAY:
- support confirmation workflows
- detect simple confirmation phrases

Future voice systems MUST NOT:
- build emotional profiles
- store conversations by default
- infer psychological states

---

# ESCALATION LIMITATION RULE

AI systems MAY:
recommend escalation.

AI systems MUST NOT:
become panic-generation systems.

Escalation should remain:
proportional,
calm,
and explainable.

---

# ADAPTIVE AI LIMITATION RULE

Future adaptive AI systems MUST remain:
- bounded
- transparent
- overrideable
- human-supervised

AI systems MUST NEVER:
evolve beyond explainable operational boundaries.

---

# SAFEHOME AI PHILOSOPHY

SafeHome AI should behave like:
a careful supportive assistant.

NOT:
an autonomous authority system.

---

# FINAL PRINCIPLE

AI capability should remain:
bounded,
transparent,
and human-centered.
